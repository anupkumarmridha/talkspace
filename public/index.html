    How this works

    Private by design. Audio and video travel straight between browsers, encrypted end-to-end with DTLS-SRTP. They never pass through a server, so nobody in the middle can listen.

    Nothing to install. Works in any modern browser. Add it to your home screen for a full-screen, app-like experience.

    Verifiable. Chat goes over the same direct peer-to-peer channel. Compare the safety number on a call to confirm nobody is in the middle.
