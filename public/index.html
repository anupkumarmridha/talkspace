    How this works

    Audio and video travel straight between browsers and are encrypted end-to-end with DTLS-SRTP — they never pass through a server, so nobody in the middle can listen. Chat goes over the same direct peer-to-peer channel, and falls back to AES-GCM ciphertext the server cannot read. Compare the safety number on a call to confirm nobody is in the middle.
